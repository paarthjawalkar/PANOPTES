PANOPTES

Created by Paarth Jawalkar. An Earth explorer optimized for Android and mobile.

Data and credits

  • CesiumJS and Cesium ion: 3D globe, world terrain, imagery access, OSM Buildings. CesiumJS is Apache-2.0 licensed; ion data and third-party assets have their own terms.
  • Bing Maps: aerial imagery through Cesium ion, subject to Bing Maps terms.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors: building and place data under ODbL.
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata: landmark search. Wikipedia text is not republished; Wikidata is CC0.
  • Nominatim: geographic search and reverse geocoding, using OpenStreetMap data.
  • ADSB.lol: live aircraft positions when available, ODbL. They may be delayed or incomplete; route lines are heading projections, not observed flight paths.
  • Nasalization by Typodermic Fonts: supplied static PANOPTES wordmark image. Body text uses system fonts.

Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles are not currently enabled. If enabled later, they will be credited here and on-map. This app is not for navigation.

Help

Search for a place, then drag and pinch to explore. Tap the compass to face north. Tap the eye to return to Earth. Hold the eye to clear the current view.

FAQ

Why are flights paused? The free data feed is unavailable right now. No flights are invented. Why do some buildings look plain? This view uses open building geometry, not photorealistic models.
