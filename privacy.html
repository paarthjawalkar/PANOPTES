PANOPTES privacy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

PANOPTES is a free, no-account web app. It is built to show you the Earth, not to collect you.

What PANOPTES itself collects

Nothing on our side. PANOPTES has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server database of its own. Your settings (quality, theme, sound) are stored only in your own browser's local storage, and clearing site data removes them.

Requests your browser makes

To draw the globe and its layers, your browser talks directly to these providers, and their own privacy policies apply:

  • Cesium ion (globe imagery, terrain, buildings) and Bing Maps aerial imagery delivered through it
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata (landmark search)
  • OpenStreetMap and Nominatim (place search and reverse geocoding - your search text goes here)
  • Open-Meteo (optional temperature layer)
  • wheretheiss.at (ISS position), satellite.js (orbit maths, runs locally)
  • NASA (ISS 3D model and, on demand, the ISS live video stream)

Analytics

If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled on this site, it is cookie-less and does not track you across sites; it counts visits and performance only.
